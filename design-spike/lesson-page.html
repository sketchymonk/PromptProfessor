Lesson Focus

What you'll master

Every high-performing prompt has four layers, stacked in a specific order: Role, Goal, Constraints, and Format. Miss any one of them and the AI guesses. When the AI guesses, you lose.

By the end of this lesson you'll be able to inspect any prompt in the wild and call out exactly which layer is weak — and fix it in under sixty seconds.

The Teach

See the four layers in action

Same request, two very different outcomes. Hover the highlighted lines on the right to see why each addition matters.

✗ Loser Prompt
write me a marketing email for our new product launch
✓ Trump Version
You are a senior B2B SaaS marketing copywriter with 10 years of experience writing for technical buyers. Write a product launch email that drives demo bookings from engineering leads at companies with 50+ engineers. Constraints: 120 words max. One specific customer-quantified outcome (e.g. 'saved 4 hrs/week'). No buzzwords (synergy, leverage, game-changer). Skeptical-reader tone. Format: - Subject line (under 8 words) - Opening hook (one sentence) - Body (2 short paragraphs) - CTA (one line, no exclamation)

Each layer lands before the next. Role first (who). Goal second (what). Constraints third (how not to). Format last (what shape). Reorder these and quality drops measurably.

Checkpoint

Do you know the stack?

Which layer is this line?
"Respond in JSON with keys: summary, sentiment, action_items."
"That's right! You're hired!"

JSON keys define the shape the AI fills in — that's Format. If the line said "only respond in JSON, never plain text," it'd be a Constraint. Subtle. Important.

Key Takeaways

The four things to remember

  • Every strong prompt has four layers: Role, Goal, Constraints, Format — in that order.
  • Role sets perspective. Goal sets outcome. Constraints prevent slop. Format defines output shape.
  • When a prompt underperforms, diagnose which layer is missing before rewriting — 80% of the time it's Constraints or Format.
  • Order matters: swapping the layers degrades results measurably across Claude, GPT, and Gemini.
Debugging Notes

Watch out for these

Common Mistake
Fused Role + Goal
"You are a marketing expert and write an email about X" — the AI treats this as one instruction and loses the specificity that comes from separating who from what.
Common Mistake
Implicit Format
Asking "summarize this" without specifying shape. The model will guess — bullets, paragraphs, numbered list — and you'll get a different shape every run. Bad for downstream parsing.
Common Mistake
Constraint inflation
Stacking more than five constraints starts to fight the Goal. If a constraint conflicts with the outcome, the model picks one and silently drops the other. Keep constraints few, sharp, and prioritized.
