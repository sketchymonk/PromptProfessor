The Challenge
Write a single prompt that turns a product's raw feature list into three LinkedIn posts — each with a different angle, each under 150 words, each ending with a call to action.

The setup: Your client is launching an AI-powered expense tool. They gave you a bullet list of features and a brand voice doc ("confident, specific, never smarmy"). Their marketing lead wants three LinkedIn posts by EOD — one for the Finance VP, one for the startup founder, one for the skeptic — and they want to run them all through one prompt, not three. You'll be graded against the R-G-C-F rubric. Every layer counts.
